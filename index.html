What this tool can and cannot tell you

What it can help with

  • Spot likely transcript groups and school-submitted materials.
  • Clarify whether the next move likely belongs to the applicant or the school.
  • Surface common blockers such as missing prior-school records or unclear upload ownership.
  • Turn a confusing situation into a checklist that is easier to review with a human.

What it cannot confirm

  • The exact official Minerva requirement list for the current cycle.
  • Whether one specific document definitely satisfies an admissions requirement.
  • Whether a submitted document has already been accepted by an official system.
  • Any judgment about admissions decisions, eligibility, or competitiveness.

Common confusion

Questions people often get stuck on

My school says it sent something, but the application still looks incomplete.

That often points to a process issue rather than a missing applicant upload. Confirm who sent the document, when it was sent, and whether the official system expects a different source or file type.

I changed schools. How do I know which transcript matters?

Start by listing each secondary school attended in the recent timeline. This tool assumes a former school may still matter if it falls within the last few years or explains part of the academic record.

I attended college or university briefly and did not finish. Could that still matter?

Possibly. This helper treats any postsecondary history as something worth checking because it may introduce another transcript source, even if the study period was short or incomplete.

We are not sure whether the school must upload documents directly.

That uncertainty alone can block progress. Use the result summary to confirm ownership before spending time requesting the wrong document from the wrong person.

Final results are not out yet. Does that mean the file must stay incomplete?

Not necessarily. Some paths rely first on a current transcript or a predicted exam document, then later on final results. This tool can only flag that timing issue as something to verify.

Glossary

Key terms used in this helper

Transcript

A school record showing coursework, grades, or both. This helper uses the term broadly and does not define the official format for Minerva.

School-submitted document

A record that may need to come directly from a counselor or school official rather than from the applicant alone.

Predicted exam documentation

A school-based record used when final exam results are not yet available. The exact document type should always be verified officially.

Final results

The final secondary-school outcome, leaving record, or exam result that may arrive after a current transcript or predicted document.

Direct upload path

A submission process where the school, counselor, or official office is expected to send a document directly.

Incomplete status

A signal that the application may still be missing a required record, a submission handoff, or a document match in the official system.

Guidance notes

Practical notes for each role

For applicants

  • Write down every school in scope before chasing documents.
  • Separate “what document exists” from “who is supposed to send it.”
  • If you are unsure, ask the school to confirm the submission path before uploading replacements.
  • Use the checklist to prepare for a conversation with admissions or the school, not as a final answer by itself.

For counselors and school officials

  • Confirm whether the file needs a current transcript, final result, predicted document, or some combination.
  • Check whether former schools or postsecondary institutions add extra record sources.
  • Make one person responsible for the official handoff whenever possible.
  • If the system still shows incomplete after sending records, compare the portal message to the exact document source and send date.
