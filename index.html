Minerva admissions helper

Transcript & School Documents Navigator

A calm, plain-language guide to help applicants and school counselors figure out which school documents may still be needed, who likely needs to send them, and where applications often get stuck.

Who it is for

Applicants, counselors, and school officials who want a fast first pass before checking official requirements.

What it helps with

Likely document lists, likely submitter roles, missing-record risks, and next-step prompts you can act on immediately.

What it does not do

It does not make admissions decisions and it does not replace official Minerva admissions guidance.

Before you start

This tool is a helper, not an official admissions policy or decision tool. Use it to organize questions and spot likely missing records, then verify every requirement against official Minerva admissions guidance.

Privacy note

Do not enter names, grades, IDs, or uploaded files here. This app saves only non-sensitive progress and checklist state in this browser.
